Support

Questions, problems or ideas: email puffling@getrackfitness.com. We aim to reply within two working days.

Restore a subscription

On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, open Puffling, tap You, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Cancel a subscription

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Puffling and tap Cancel subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

Reminders do not arrive

Check that notifications for Puffling are on in iPhone Settings, under Notifications, and that a Focus mode is not silencing them. Then set the time again in the app under You, Reminder.

No sound

Turn on Sound under You, pick a sound before you start, and check your volume. Sounds play even with the silent switch on.

Delete your data

Tap You, then Delete my data. Puffling keeps everything on your iPhone, so this removes it completely. See the Privacy Policy.
