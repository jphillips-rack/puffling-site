Puffling

Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

In short: Puffling does not collect any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no tracking, no advertising and no analytics.

Who we are

Puffling is made by Jamie Philp, an independent developer in the United Kingdom ("we"). For anything about privacy, email support@getpuffling.com.

What stays on your iPhone

To work, the app stores the following on your device only:

  • your pufferfish's name, and your answer to "What would you like help with?"
  • your settings, such as your reminder time, daily goal, sound and haptics
  • your breathing history: sessions, minutes, streak, XP and plan progress
  • any custom breathing patterns you make

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it. If you use iCloud or iTunes backups, it is included in your device backup, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for an account, your email address or your name.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not use your microphone, camera, location, contacts or Apple Health.
  • We do not sell or share any data, because we do not collect any.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app checks with Apple, on your device, whether you have an active subscription, so it can unlock paid features.

Reminders

If you turn on a reminder, it is scheduled on your iPhone as a local notification. Nothing is sent to a server. You can turn it off in the app or in iPhone Settings.

Deleting your data

Tap You, then Delete my data, or delete the app. Either removes everything Puffling stored on your iPhone. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; see Support for how to cancel.

Children

Puffling is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal data, it does not collect data from children.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over your personal data. As we hold none, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete, but you can contact us with any question. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top. If we ever start collecting data, we will say so in the app before we do.
